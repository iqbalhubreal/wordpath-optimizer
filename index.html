GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

The essentials about optimization, AI generation, and your data.

Does WordPath send my entire word list to Gemini?

No. The optimizer runs locally in your browser. Only the words on a specific chain card are sent to Google when you choose to generate its crossword title and clues.

Where is my API key stored?

The page does not save your key to browser storage or send it to a WordPath server. It is used in a direct request from your browser to Google's Gemini API. Do not share a device while your key is entered.

Why might Gemini fail to connect?

Check your internet connection, API key permissions, model access, and quota. Use a current key from Google AI Studio, and make sure your key is allowed to call the Gemini API.

Are the generated chains guaranteed to be optimal?

No. WordPath tries multiple arrangements and keeps the best result found during those passes. A deeper run can find a better arrangement, but results can vary between runs.
